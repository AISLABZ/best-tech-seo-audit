Critical findings

Two issues. Each loses customers today.

Critical

1 · www.best-tech.co.uk does not resolve

The www hostname returned no DNS record (ENOTFOUND) from two independent resolvers. Only the bare domain, best-tech.co.uk, answers. Anyone who types the www form, or follows a printed or third-party link that uses it, sees a browser error. The address submitted for this audit was the www form.

Fix: Webflow already lists www.best-tech.co.uk as a custom domain, so only the DNS record is missing. At the DNS provider for the domain, add CNAME www → cdn.webflow.com (or the exact target Webflow → Site settings → Publishing shows), keep best-tech.co.uk as the default domain so www 301-redirects to it, then confirm in a browser.

Critical

2 · AI engines describe Best Tech from a third-party listing, not its own site

best-tech.co.uk was not cited in any of the five tests. When the brand was found, the source was a magicpin directory page showing 11 ratings and a service mix (console repair, SSD upgrades, euro and dollar payments) that doesn't match the site's own positioning. The engine itself called 11 ratings "a thin basis".

Fix: build the off-site entity footprint AI engines retrieve from. Steps: claim and complete the magicpin profile; list on Yell, Apple Business Connect, Bing Places, Trustpilot and Checkatrade with identical name, address and phone details; add those URLs to the sameAs array in the homepage schema; and run a review drive (see finding 7). In GA4, add an "AI assistants" channel group so referrals from chatgpt.com, perplexity.ai, gemini.google.com and copilot.microsoft.com show up separately.

High-priority findings

Six issues that directly limit rankings or AI citation.

High

3 · No prices are published anywhere, not even on the pricing guide

None of the 73 pages contains a £ figure. That includes /post/transparent-repair-pricing-guide-london-devices and /post/macbook-screen-repair-cost. Cost questions ("how much is an iPhone 15 screen in London?") are among the most common repair prompts, and AI engines answer them only from sources that publish numbers.

Fix: add a "from £" price table by device and model (top 15 models) to the pricing guide and to each /works/ page, with a line saying the firm quote is confirmed after the free diagnostic. Put the same figures into the Offer schema as priceSpecification.

High

4 · The homepage H1 is a seasonal slogan — fixed 21 Oct

The homepage's only H1 reads Cold mornings kill tired batteries. The H1 is the strongest on-page statement of what the business is and where. This one names neither the service nor the location.

Fix: make the H1 permanent, for example "Phone & Laptop Repair inside Piccadilly Circus Station". Keep the seasonal line as a styled paragraph or H2 above it.

Done: the H1 now reads "Phone & Laptop Repair inside Piccadilly Circus Station" and is live. The seasonal battery message stays in the paragraph below it.

High

5 · 59 articles carry no publish or update date — schema fixed 21 Oct

The BlogPosting schema on every post omits datePublished and dateModified, and no date is shown on the page. AI engines and Google weigh freshness, and model-specific posts (iPhone 17, Pixel 10, Galaxy S25) lose value when they carry no date.

Fix: bind the CMS "Published On" and "Updated On" fields into the JSON-LD embed and show "Updated [date]" under each post title.

Done (schema): the dates were already in the CMS but reached the page only through a JavaScript-injected block, which AI crawlers that don't run JavaScript never see. The server-rendered BlogPosting block on the Blog Posts template now carries datePublished (CMS Published Date) and dateModified (last CMS update), and the one undated post was given its creation date. Still open: a visible "Updated" line under post titles, which needs a Designer edit.

High

6 · No named expert behind any of the content

All 59 posts list the author as the Organization "Best Tech Repair". Nothing names a technician or states qualifications, years at the bench, or repair volumes. This is the experience signal both Google's quality guidelines and AI engines use to choose which source to trust.

Fix: create a technician profile page for the lead technician (photo, experience, certifications, devices specialised in). Change the post author to a Person linked to that page, and add a short "Checked by" line to each post.

High

7 · The "Rated 5.0 on Google" claim has no review count

The homepage title, description and social cards all say "Rated 5.0 on Google". Third-party sources show 11 ratings. A rating with no count reads as weak evidence to both people and AI engines, and the engine flagged it explicitly.

Fix: ask every customer for a review at handover (QR code at the counter, plus a WhatsApp follow-up link). Once there are 50 or more, state the count ("5.0 from 50+ Google reviews"). Aim for reviews that mention the device and repair type.

High

8 · The 4K hero video is still served to phones

The same 3840 × 2160 30fps master clip found in August (5.78 MB MP4 then) is still embedded on 14 pages, including the homepage and every service page. In August this drove mobile LCP to 9.0s. A fresh PageSpeed run was rate-limited during this audit, so mobile vitals were not re-measured.

Fix: re-encode to 720p H.264 under 1 MB, set a WebP poster, and swap it for the poster image alone on mobile.

What is already working for GEO

Keep these intact through future site changes.

  1. All seven AI and search crawlers tested are served the full page (GPTBot, OAI-SearchBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended, CCBot, Bingbot), with no Cloudflare challenge. robots.txt allows everything and points to the sitemap.
  2. llms.txt is exemplary. It gives a one-paragraph entity definition, explicitly separates the shop from generic "best tech" review sites, and lists key facts, both phone numbers with their roles, and a curated link map.
  3. The LocalBusiness schema is complete: a stable @id, geo-coordinates, a Google Maps CID, hours, two typed contact points, and Service offers referenced from every service page by @id.
  4. Every title and description is unique across all 73 pages, and every page has exactly one H1 and a self-referencing canonical.

Method & limitations

All 73 URLs in the live sitemap were fetched on 21 October 2026 (the CMS holds further published posts the cached sitemap does not yet list) and all returned HTTP 200. Headings, meta tags, canonicals, alt text, and JSON-LD were parsed directly from the HTML. Analytics was confirmed from the Webflow site scripts after an inline-only check missed the hosted GA4 script. Every count above comes from that parse. AI crawler access was tested by requesting the homepage with each bot's user-agent string. This confirms the server response. It does not confirm what each vendor later indexes. The prompt tests used a search-grounded AI answer engine as a proxy for ChatGPT Search, Perplexity and AI Overviews. Answers vary by platform, location and day, so treat the score of 50 as a directional baseline, not a ranking. The www DNS failure was observed from two resolvers and should be confirmed at the registrar. Not covered: a fresh Lighthouse run (rate-limited), backlink profile, keyword rankings, Google Business Profile insights, and direct ChatGPT, Gemini or Claude prompt sampling. A brand-tracking connector (Ahrefs Brand Radar) was configured but failed to connect during this session.
