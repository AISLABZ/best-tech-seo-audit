## Laptop repair — `/works/laptop-repair`

**Which laptops do you repair?**
We repair Apple MacBook Air and MacBook Pro, plus Windows laptops from Dell, HP, Lenovo, Acer, ASUS,
Toshiba and Microsoft Surface. Common jobs include cracked or flickering screens, batteries, charging and
power faults, and software problems such as slow performance, crashes and virus removal.

**Do I need an appointment, or can I walk in?**
Walk-ins are welcome any day between 10am and 10pm at Unit 4, inside Piccadilly Circus Underground
Station. You can also book online, which puts your laptop straight onto the bench when you arrive.

**How long does a laptop repair take?**
Most laptop repairs are done the same day. The technician confirms the fault, the part and the
turnaround at the free diagnostic, before any work starts, so you know exactly when to collect
before you commit to anything.

**How much does a laptop repair cost?**
It depends on the make, model and fault. The diagnostic is free, you get a firm fixed price before any
work begins, and there are no hidden fees. If we cannot fix it, you pay nothing: no fix, no fee.

**Will I lose my files?**
Hardware repairs such as screens, batteries and charging ports do not touch your storage, and our
technicians never access, copy or compromise your data. If a software repair could affect your files,
we talk you through backing up first. If files are already lost, our data recovery is no recovery, no fee.

**Is it worth repairing an older laptop? ⚑**
If the laptop was fast enough for you before the fault, a repair is usually the sensible choice. The
free diagnostic gives you a firm price, so you can compare it with the cost of a new machine before
deciding.

## Tablet & iPad repair — `/works/tablet-repair`

**Which tablets do you repair?**
We repair every iPad model, including iPad Air, iPad mini and iPad Pro, as well as Samsung Galaxy Tab,
Microsoft Surface Go, Amazon Fire and other Android tablets. Common repairs are cracked screens,
batteries, charging ports and liquid damage.

**Can you replace just the cracked glass on my iPad? ⚑**
On some iPads the glass and touch layer are separate from the display underneath, so cracked glass can
be replaced on its own. On others the screen is a single bonded unit. We check your model at the free
diagnostic and tell you which repair it needs.

**How long does a tablet repair take?**
Most tablet repairs are done the same day. Timing depends on the make and model, and we confirm the
turnaround at the free diagnostic, before any work begins.

**How much does a tablet repair cost?**
The price depends on your tablet's make, model and fault. The diagnostic is free, you get a firm fixed
price before any work starts, and if we cannot fix it you pay nothing: no fix, no fee.

**My tablet got wet. What should I do? ⚑**
Switch it off, do not charge it, and bring it in as soon as you can. Our technicians inspect and repair
liquid-damaged tablets to recover them wherever possible, and the sooner we see it, the better the
chances.

**Where are you?**
Best Tech Repair, Unit 4, Piccadilly Circus Underground Station, London W1J 9HS, two minutes from
Regent Street. Open 10am to 10pm, seven days a week. No appointment needed. Call 020 3417 7090.

## Smartwatch repair — `/works/smartwatch-repair`

**Which smartwatches do you repair?**
We repair all major smartwatch brands: Apple Watch (all series), Samsung Galaxy Watch, Garmin, Fitbit,
Huawei Watch, Fossil, Amazfit and more. Our technicians are trained to work on the very small components
inside smartwatches.

**What smartwatch faults can you fix?**
We replace cracked or unresponsive screens and worn batteries, repair stuck buttons and digital crowns,
fix cable and wireless charging faults, assess water damage, and resolve software problems such as a
frozen screen, a failed update or a watch that will not pair with your phone.

**How long does a smartwatch repair take?**
Most smartwatch repairs are completed the same day, so you are not without your watch for long. We
confirm the turnaround at the free diagnostic, before any work begins.

**How much does a smartwatch repair cost?**
It depends on the watch and the fault. The diagnostic is free, you get a firm fixed price before any work
starts, and there are no hidden fees. If we cannot fix it, you pay nothing: no fix, no fee.

**My watch got wet and stopped working. Can you help?**
Bring it in as soon as possible. Our technicians assess the damage and recover what they can, and the
sooner we see it, the better the outcome is likely to be.

**Do I need an appointment?**
No. Walk in any day between 10am and 10pm at Unit 4, inside Piccadilly Circus Underground Station,
or book online so your watch goes straight onto the bench when you arrive.
