## Screen replacement — `/services/screen-replacement`

**How long does a screen replacement take?**
Most screens are replaced in about 45 minutes while you wait, and many repairs are finished in under
an hour. If your model needs longer, we tell you at the free diagnostic, before you commit to anything.

**Which devices can you replace screens on?**
Phones (every iPhone, Samsung Galaxy, Google Pixel, OnePlus, Huawei), laptops (MacBook Air and Pro,
Dell, HP, Lenovo, Acer, ASUS, Toshiba, Surface), tablets (every iPad, Galaxy Tab, Surface Go, Amazon
Fire) and smartwatches (every Apple Watch series, Galaxy Watch, Garmin, Fitbit, Fossil).

**How much does a screen replacement cost?**
It depends on the device and the screen type. The technician confirms the exact fault, the screen your
device needs and a firm fixed price at the free diagnostic, before any work begins. If we cannot fix
it, you pay nothing.

**Is there a warranty?**
Yes. Screen replacements carry a 90-day warranty on the part and our workmanship. Before handing your
device back, we test touch sensitivity, brightness, colour accuracy and the surrounding functions.

**Will I lose my photos or data?**
No. A screen replacement does not touch your storage, and our technicians never access, copy or
compromise your data.

## Battery replacement — `/services/battery-replacement`

**How do I know if my battery needs replacing?**
Fast drain, sudden shutdowns with charge still showing, or a device that will not last the day are the
usual signs. Our free battery health check measures the battery's health, charge cycle count and
capacity, and we tell you plainly whether a replacement is needed.

**How long does a battery replacement take?**
Most battery replacements are fitted while you wait, and many are completed in under an hour. Walk in
any day between 10am and 10pm; no appointment is needed.

**What battery do you fit?**
A high-quality replacement matched to your device's original specification: safe voltage, correct
capacity and full compatibility with its charging system. After fitting, we test charge intake,
voltage stability and that the battery reads correctly in your settings.

**Is there a warranty?**
Yes. Every battery replacement comes with a 90-day warranty on the battery and labour. If an issue
related to the replacement arises in that time, we sort it at no extra cost.

**My battery is swollen. What should I do? ⚑**
Stop charging the device and bring it in as soon as you can. A swollen battery is a safety issue, and
our free diagnostic will confirm whether it needs replacing.

## Camera & speaker repair — `/services/camera-speaker-repair`

**What camera and speaker faults do you fix?**
Rear cameras that will not focus, cracked front cameras, earpieces that are barely audible on calls,
distorted loudspeakers and microphones nobody can hear. We first check whether the cause is hardware,
software or simply dust and debris.

**How long does a camera or speaker repair take?**
Most are done the same day. You get a clear explanation and a firm fixed price at the free diagnostic,
before anything is touched.

**What parts do you use?**
Camera modules and speaker components sourced to OEM specification and matched to your device model.
After the repair we test focus, image quality, flash, video, speaker volume and clarity, microphone
input and call audio.

**Is there a warranty?**
Yes. Every camera and speaker repair comes with a 90-day parts and labour warranty.

**Do I need an appointment?**
No. Walk in any day between 10am and 10pm at Unit 4, inside Piccadilly Circus Underground Station,
or book a slot online.

## Software fixing — `/services/software-fixing`

**What software problems can you fix?**
Freezing, crashing and slow devices; operating system reinstalls and recovery for Windows, macOS, iOS
and Android; malware removal and antivirus setup; and lock-outs from devices and accounts. We find the
root cause rather than recommending new hardware you do not need.

**Will a software repair delete my files?**
Not if we can avoid it. We can safely back up and transfer your data before any reinstall, and we talk
you through backing up first. Our technicians never access, copy or compromise your data.

**Can you remove a virus?**
Yes. We remove malware and set up antivirus and safe browsing on Windows, macOS, iOS and Android, with
practical advice on keeping your accounts and passwords secure.

**How long does a software repair take?**
Most software repairs are done the same day. We confirm the turnaround at the free diagnostic.

**How much does it cost?**
It depends on the device and the problem. The diagnostic is free, the price is agreed before any work
starts, and there are no hidden fees. If we cannot fix it, you pay nothing.

## Data recovery — `/services/data-recovery`

**Can you recover data from a phone that will not turn on?**
Often, yes. In most cases the data is still recoverable. We use professional-grade tools to retrieve
photos, documents, contacts and files from damaged, corrupted or accidentally wiped devices.

**Which devices can you recover data from?**
iPhone, Android, iPad, MacBook, Windows laptops, external hard drives, SSDs, USB drives, SD cards and
more, including drives that click or are not detected.

**What does data recovery cost?**
You only pay if we successfully recover your data. If we cannot get it back, you pay nothing: no
recovery, no fee. The diagnostic is free.

**My device got wet. What should I do?**
Bring it in immediately. The sooner we see a water-damaged or physically broken device, the better
the chances of a full recovery.

**Is my data kept confidential? ⚑**
Yes. Recovery is done here in London, and our technicians never access, copy or compromise your data
beyond what is needed to recover it for you.
