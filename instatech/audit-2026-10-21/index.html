Critical findings

Three issues, each affecting rankings or conversion at scale.

Critical

1 · Eighteen products are published twice

The catalogue contains 18 product pairs where a second copy sits at a -2 slug — e.g. /product/a1-aux-cable-1m/ and /product/a1-aux-cable-1m-2/. Each pair has the same title, description, price and images (re-uploaded as …-1.png); only the assigned category differs (“Audio Cables” vs “Audio”). Both copies are self-canonical and both are in product-sitemap.xml, so 36 URLs compete against each other for the same queries.

Fix: keep one product per pair, assign it to both categories (WooCommerce supports multiple), delete the duplicate and 301 it to the survivor via Rank Math → Redirections. Affected: ethernet cables (4), audio splitters and adapters (6), Z-series audio cables (3), smart-watch cables (2), USB-C HDMI, QUATRE hub, A1 AUX, World Travel Adapter.

Critical

2 · Mobile homepage takes 10 seconds to show anything

Local Lighthouse 12 mobile run (simulated Moto G Power, slow 4G): performance 27 / 100, First Contentful Paint 10.3 s, Largest Contentful Paint 11.1 s, Speed Index 10.3 s. Desktop scores 67 with LCP 3.2 s. The homepage is the main landing page for “phone repair London” traffic.

Fix: see findings 3 and 4 — the stylesheet bundle and main-thread JavaScript account for nearly all of it. Target mobile LCP ≤ 2.5 s.

Critical

3 · One 729 KB stylesheet blocks every page render

LiteSpeed Cache’s “CSS Combine” produces a single /wp-content/litespeed/css/…css of 729 KB transferred — a third of the homepage’s 2,217 KiB weight — which the browser must download and parse before painting. On top of it the theme loads three separate icon fonts: ElementsKit icon pack (259 KB), Fontello (125 KB) and Font Awesome Solid (79 KB).

Fix: in LiteSpeed Cache → Page Optimization turn on Generate Critical CSS and Load CSS Asynchronously; disable unused Elementor/ElementsKit widgets so their CSS leaves the bundle; consolidate to one icon set (or inline SVGs) and drop the other two.

High-priority findings

Eight issues actively costing rankings, rich results or local visibility.

High

4 · Main thread is busy for 18.9 seconds on mobile

Total Blocking Time 2,790 ms (good ≤ 200 ms); script evaluation 3.7 s; 168 KB of unused JavaScript; DOM of 2,412 elements. The mega-menu repeats the full product-category tree in markup on every page.

Fix: enable LiteSpeed “Delay JS” for non-critical scripts (gtag, chat, sliders); trim the mega-menu to top-level categories; remove unused Elementor add-ons.

High

5 · Uncached pages take ~3 seconds to start responding

Cache-busted requests to six representative URLs returned the first byte in 2.77–3.18 s; the desktop Lighthouse run recorded 2,810 ms for the root document. Cached copies from Hostinger’s CDN arrive in 0.22–0.48 s, so first visitors after a purge, and Googlebot on less-visited product URLs, get the slow path. Crawling all 276 sitemap URLs averaged 9.2 s per page under modest concurrency.

Fix: enable LiteSpeed Crawler to pre-warm the cache after purges; enable Object Cache (Redis/Memcached on Hostinger); audit slow plugins with Query Monitor.

High

6 · Product schema is too thin for Google’s merchant listings

All 159 product pages emit Rank Math Product + Offer with price and stock, but none include brand, sku, gtin/mpn, shippingDetails, hasMerchantReturnPolicy or reviews. The product name also carries the “ - InstaTech” title suffix.

Fix: populate SKU and GTIN in WooCommerce inventory fields; set a brand attribute; add shipping and returns policy in Rank Math → Schema → WooCommerce (or in Google Merchant Center).

High

7 · The business is described as three different entities

The homepage marks up MobilePhoneStore “InstaTech Bloomsbury” with @id /locations/#bloomsbury but url /locations/. The Bloomsbury page itself publishes a separate Store called “instaTech Bloomsbury” with no @id, a differently formatted phone number and a parent organisation “instaTech”, while the site-wide Organization is “InstaTech”. Google cannot reliably join these to one business or to the Google Business Profiles.

Fix: one LocalBusiness node per shop with a stable @id (e.g. /locations/bloomsbury/#store), url pointing to that shop’s page, parentOrganization → /#organization, identical name, phone and hours to the GBP listing. Reference — don’t redeclare — it from the homepage and service pages.

High

8 · 64 URLs have no meta description

Missing on 33 of 33 product-category pages, 26 of 159 products and 5 of 6 blog categories. A further 56 URLs share a description with another URL (largely the duplicate products).

Fix: Rank Math → Titles & Meta → Product Categories: set a description template (e.g. “Shop %term% at InstaTech — collect today in Bloomsbury or Moorgate…”), then hand-write the top ten categories.

High

9 · 28% of images have no alt attribute

972 of 3,449 <img> elements across the site lack alt text: 791 of 2,387 on product pages (gallery thumbnails and related-product tiles), 125 of 489 on blog posts, 33 on product categories.

Fix: fill alt text in the Media Library for product gallery images (WooCommerce re-uses it everywhere); Rank Math → Images → “Add missing ALT attributes” using %title% as an interim fallback.

High

10 · The homepage H1 is a promotion, not a keyword

The only H1 on the homepage is “2 shops weren’t enough. Oxford Circus is next.” — the page’s strongest relevance signal says nothing about phone, laptop or repair. The title tag (“Phone & Laptop Repair London – Same Day”) is well targeted; the H1 should match it.

Fix: make the H1 “Phone & Laptop Repair in Central London — Same Day” and move the Oxford Circus announcement to an H2 or banner.

High

11 · The Oxford Circus page still says “opening soon”

Title “Phone Repair Oxford Circus W1 – Opening Soon”; copy says “opening October 2026”, and the site-wide banner repeats it. The audit date is 21 October 2026. If the shop is open, the page is under-selling it; if not, the date needs to move.

Fix: on opening, retitle to “Phone Repair Oxford Circus W1”, switch its schema from WebPage to a LocalBusiness node with hours, add it to /locations/ and to the homepage store list, and claim the Google Business Profile.

Where to start

Ordered by impact per hour of work.

  1. Merge the 18 duplicate products and 301 each -2 URL to its original. Under an hour in WooCommerce + Rank Math Redirections.
  2. Turn on Critical CSS and async CSS in LiteSpeed Cache, then re-test. This is the single largest lever on the 10-second mobile paint.
  3. Enable the LiteSpeed crawler and object cache so uncached responses stop costing 3 seconds.
  4. Unify the local-business schema — one node per shop, one spelling of the brand, matching the Google Business Profiles.
  5. Rewrite the homepage H1 to target “phone & laptop repair London”, and update the Oxford Circus page to reflect its actual status.
  6. Set Rank Math description templates for product categories and blog categories; add brand, SKU and GTIN to products.
  7. Consolidate icon fonts and delay non-critical JavaScript to bring Total Blocking Time under 600 ms.
  8. Work through product-image alt text in the Media Library, starting with the best-selling cables and chargers.

Method & limitations

All 276 URLs listed in the Rank Math sitemap index (17 pages, 61 posts, 159 products, 33 product categories, 6 blog categories) were fetched and parsed on 21 October 2026; every count above comes from that parse. Performance figures are single Lighthouse 12.2 lab runs (mobile and desktop presets) against the homepage from a cloud host; lab scores vary by several points between runs and locations. Server timings were measured with cache-busting query strings on six URLs. Product schema completeness was checked on all 159 products. Not covered: WordPress admin settings, backlink profile, keyword rankings, competitor comparison, Google Business Profile listings and field Core Web Vitals.
