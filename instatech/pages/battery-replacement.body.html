Is it really the battery?

Tap what your phone is doing

Battery, charging port and software faults can look alike. The free diagnostic tells us which one it is before any work starts.

Battery cell

The battery has worn out

Every lithium battery loses capacity with each charge cycle. When a full charge no longer gets you through the day, a new battery is usually the fix. On an iPhone, Settings › Battery › Battery Health shows how far it has dropped.

20 to 40 min Free diagnostic Warranty
Battery cell

The battery can no longer hold up under load

A worn battery reports charge left but cannot deliver the power the phone asks for, so it switches off early, often in the cold or when the camera starts. A new battery almost always solves it.

20 to 40 min Free diagnostic Warranty
Swollen battery · urgent

Stop charging it and bring it in

A lifting back or screen usually means the battery has swollen. Switch the phone off, keep it away from heat and do not press the case back together. Bring it to either shop as soon as you can and we will remove the battery safely and fit a new one.

Same day Free diagnostic Warranty
Battery or charging circuit

Usually the battery, sometimes the charging circuit

An ageing battery runs hot as it struggles to charge. A damaged cable, charger or port can cause heat too. We test the battery and the charging path on the bench, so you only replace what has actually failed.

Free diagnostic Same day Warranty
Charging port first

Often the port, not the battery

Fluff in the charging port or a worn connector is a common cause, and it is a quicker fix than a battery. We check the port first in the free diagnostic, then the battery. See our charging port repair.
