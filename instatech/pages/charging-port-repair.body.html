Charging port

The contacts are worn or blocked

If the cable only works held at an angle, the pins inside the port are no longer making a clean contact. Packed lint is the most common reason; worn or bent pins are the other. We clean first, then replace the port if the contacts are damaged.

Charging port

Lint is stopping the plug seating

Pocket fluff compresses into a hard layer at the back of the port, so the plug cannot click fully in. A careful clean with the right tools often fixes it. Please don't dig in with a pin or a metal clip: it can short or bend the contacts.

Port, cable or battery

We test each part on the bench

A phone that will not charge at all can have a failed port, a damaged cable or charger, or a battery that has stopped taking charge. We test the whole charging path, so you only pay for what has actually failed. If it is the battery, see our battery replacement.

Cable, charger or port

Often the cable, sometimes the port

Try a different cable and a wall charger you know works. If it is still slow, a damaged pin in the port can stop fast charging from engaging. The free diagnostic shows which part is at fault.

Liquid or debris

Unplug it and let the port dry

A moisture or "accessory not supported" warning usually means liquid or debris in the port. Unplug the cable and don't force it to charge. If the warning stays once the phone is dry, the port may be corroding. Bring it in, and see our water damage repair.
