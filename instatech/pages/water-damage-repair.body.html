First aid

Tap what happened

The first hour matters most. Here is what to do, and what not to do, before you bring it in.

Act now

Switch it off and keep it off

Take it out, power it off, and don't charge it or press buttons to test it. Remove the case and SIM tray, dry the outside with a lint-free cloth and bring it in. Skip the rice: it doesn't draw water out of the phone and can leave dust and starch in the ports.

Bring it in today Free diagnostic Warranty
Sugary or salty liquid

Drinks corrode faster than water

Coffee, juice, beer and sea water leave sugar and salt behind, which corrode the board faster than clean water. Switch it off, don't charge it, and bring it in as soon as you can so we can clean the residue out.

Bring it in today Free diagnostic Warranty
Charging port

Unplug it and let the port dry

A moisture warning means liquid is sitting in the port. Unplug the cable and don't force it to charge. If the warning stays once the phone is dry, the port may be corroding. See our charging port repair.

Free diagnostic Port cleaned first Warranty
Hidden damage

Working now doesn't mean it's fine

Speakers, cameras, Face ID and the screen can fail days later as corrosion spreads. If your phone got wet and is now glitching, back it up and bring it in for the free diagnostic before more parts are affected.

Bring it in today Data checked first Warranty
No power

Don't keep trying to switch it on

Repeatedly powering a wet phone can short the board. Leave it off and bring it in. We check the board and the battery, and if the phone can't be saved we can tell you whether your photos and contacts can be recovered.
