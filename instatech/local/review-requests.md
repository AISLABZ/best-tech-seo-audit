# Review requests (backlog 3.3)

Reviews are the biggest lever for the map results. Ask **every** customer after **every** repair.

**Google policy (non-negotiable):**
- No incentives (no discounts, gifts or prize draws).
- No "review gating", meaning you never ask only happy customers or filter by satisfaction first.
- Never write reviews for the business, and never ask staff or family to.
- Reply to every review, good and bad, within 48 hours.

**Review links** (backlog 1.17):
- Bloomsbury: https://search.google.com/local/writereview?placeid=ChIJg5Ii0NUbdkgRQL_2omA3r5M
- Moorgate: https://search.google.com/local/writereview?placeid=ChIJm5ZiHwAddkgRICvcMAd8o3Q
- Oxford Circus: add once the listing exists.

Tip: shorten each link in Google Business Profile → "Ask for reviews" (it gives a short g.page link), and use that in SMS so the message stays within one 160-character text.

---

## 1. SMS: send when the customer collects (or within 2 hours)

**Bloomsbury (≤160 characters with a short link)**
> Thanks for choosing InstaTech Bloomsbury today. If you have a minute, a Google review really helps us: [short link]. Reply STOP to opt out.

**Moorgate**
> Thanks for choosing InstaTech Moorgate today. If you have a minute, a Google review really helps us: [short link]. Reply STOP to opt out.

Send only to customers who gave their number for the repair, and honour opt-outs (PECR). Message once only, with no reminders.

---

## 2. Email: for customers who left an email address

**Subject:** Thanks for visiting InstaTech [Bloomsbury | Moorgate]

> Hi [first name],
>
> Thanks for bringing your [device] to InstaTech [Bloomsbury | Moorgate]. We hope it's working perfectly.
>
> If you have a minute, we'd really value a Google review. It helps people nearby find a repair shop they can trust:
> [review link]
>
> If anything isn't right with the repair, just reply to this email or pop back in. Every repair carries a warranty on parts and labour.
>
> Thanks,
> The InstaTech [Bloomsbury | Moorgate] team
> [shop address] · [shop phone]

(The warranty line meets Google's rules because it goes to everyone and is not conditional on the review.)

## 3. In-store: counter card and receipt line

**Counter card (A6, with a QR code to the shop's review link)**
> **Fixed today? Tell London.**
> Scan to leave us a Google review. It takes a minute and helps neighbours find us.
> [QR code] · InstaTech [Bloomsbury | Moorgate]

**Receipt / job sheet footer**
> Happy with your repair? Review us on Google: [short link]

**What staff say at handover:** "If you're happy with it, a quick Google review really helps us. There's a QR code right here." Say it to every customer, not selectively.

---

## 4. Replying to reviews (templates; personalise every reply)

**Positive**
> Thanks [name]! Glad your [device] is back to full strength. See you next time at [Bloomsbury | Moorgate].

**Negative**
> Hi [name], I'm sorry the repair didn't meet your expectations. Every repair carries a warranty on parts and labour, so please bring the [device] back, or call us on [shop phone], and we'll put it right.

Never argue, never share customer details, and keep any refund discussion offline.
