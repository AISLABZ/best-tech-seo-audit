Four directions for the Mensfe forum

Each direction redesigns the same forum around the same content: a forum-first homepage, the board index, a topic page and a medical information page, all mobile-ready and built to fix the issues in the SEO audit. Directions A–C present the forum through a modern website front; Direction D keeps the look and feel of a true message board. Please pick one direction (or the parts you like from each) and we will develop it into the full site and SMF theme.

All three share the same fixes: one clear heading per page, real text instead of text in images, latest discussions on the homepage, the founders' credentials on show, reviewer and date on medical pages, accessible contrast, and layouts that work on phones. Member counts appear as [n] placeholders, and sample posts are illustrative.

Direction A

Harbour: bold and branded

Personality

  • The deep sea-teal of the current shoreline banner, with a sunset-gold call to action
  • A strong, memorable hero; the forum is visible from the first screen
  • Best if Mensfe wants to look established and confident

Colour and type

  • Manrope headings, Atkinson Hyperlegible body text (designed for low-vision readability)

Watch-outs

  • Dark bands are heavier on long pages
  • Gold needs disciplined use to stay special

Direction B

Clear: clean and clinical

Personality

  • Calm, trustworthy and information-dense, like a modern health service
  • A forum "app" layout: boards in a sidebar, a live topic feed with Latest, Unanswered and Top
  • Best if Mensfe wants to feel medically credible and efficient

Colour and type

  • Plus Jakarta Sans headings, Source Sans 3 body text

Watch-outs

  • Can feel less personal; relies on activity in the feed
  • Closest to SMF's own structure, so the easiest to theme

Direction C

Stories: warm and human

Personality

  • Editorial and reassuring; members' own words take centre stage
  • Generous spacing and a serif voice for a reflective, magazine feel
  • Best if Mensfe wants to emphasise empathy and the emotional side

Colour and type

  • Fraunces headings, Nunito Sans body text

Watch-outs

  • Needs members' consent for featured stories
  • Less dense, so more scrolling on busy boards

Direction D

Forum: a true message board, reimagined

Personality

  • The board index is the homepage, so regulars land straight in the forum
  • Classic forum anatomy (category bars, last-post columns, profile panels beside posts, quick reply, info center) with a modern dark finish
  • An illustrated night-shoreline banner revives "You'll never walk alone at Mensfe"

Colour and type

  • Bricolage Grotesque headings, Source Sans 3 body text; each category has its own colour

Watch-outs

  • Dark themes divide opinion; a light variant can be offered as a member setting in SMF
  • Closest to SMF's own templates, so it is the quickest to build
