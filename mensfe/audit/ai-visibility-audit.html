How AI assistants see Mensfe

More people now ask ChatGPT, Google's AI Overviews, Perplexity, Gemini, Copilot and Claude where to find support instead of scrolling search results. This audit looks at whether those systems find Mensfe, how they describe it, and what would make them recommend and cite it.

Summary

AI assistants recommend sources they can find (crawlable, indexed pages), understand (a clear entity with consistent facts) and trust (visible expertise, freshness and other sites vouching for them). Mensfe is findable for its own name, and when it does appear the AI describes it accurately, but it repeats Mensfe's dated meta description almost word for word. For the questions a worried man is likely to ask ("where can I get support for azoospermia?"), Mensfe is currently absent. The answers go to Mumsnet, Fertility Network UK's HIMfertility, counselling bodies and private clinics.

Mensfe's strongest asset for AI visibility is one these systems value highly: 18+ years of authentic, first-hand forum discussion by men, run by a fertility counsellor and a medical scientist with lived experience of azoospermia. The work is to make that asset readable, attributable and referenced.

Top five actions

  1. Fix the technical blockers from the SEO audit first: HTTPS, a single domain, mobile-friendliness and Bing Webmaster Tools. ChatGPT search and Copilot draw on Bing's index, Google's AI Overviews on Google's.
  2. Define the Mensfe entity: one name, one description, Organization and Person schema, consistent profiles and directory listings.
  3. Rewrite the homepage and meta descriptions in the words you want AI to repeat ("free, anonymous peer support forum for men facing infertility, founded in 2007 by…").
  4. Make forum knowledge citable: clean topic URLs, DiscussionForumPosting schema, and "community wisdom" summary pages that answer the most common questions.
  5. Earn mentions on the sites AI already trusts for this topic: Fertility Network UK, BICA, NHS-adjacent and clinic resource pages, Mumsnet discussions and podcasts.
Method and limits. We ran five realistic prompts through a web search engine of the kind AI assistants use to ground their answers, and recorded which sources were returned and how the AI-generated summary described Mensfe. The site itself could not be crawled from our environment, so on-site signals come from the homepage source supplied by the client. AI answers vary by tool, location, date and phrasing. Treat this as a baseline snapshot, and repeat it monthly with the tracking set in section 5.

What this tells us

  • The brand is known but not chosen. AI systems can describe Mensfe accurately when asked about it directly, but it is rarely what they reach for when someone describes their situation.
  • AI repeats your own words. The meta description written years ago (with "Undescended testicles" and "ICSI" up front) is effectively Mensfe's AI-era elevator pitch. It should be rewritten deliberately.
  • Peer-support answers already favour forums. Mumsnet threads rank for azoospermia support. Mensfe's forum is exactly this kind of content, made by men, but it is not being surfaced.
  • Competitors in AI answers: Fertility Network UK / HIMfertility, Mumsnet Infertility, Men's Fertility Support (Facebook group), RESOLVE (US), TRIO Fertility, Fertilicare and BICA.

3. Findings and fixes

A1Make the site crawlable by search and AI botsCritical
Why
AI answers are grounded in search indexes and their own crawlers. Bing powers ChatGPT search and Copilot; Google powers AI Overviews and Gemini. Pages that are duplicated, insecure or hard to render are cited less.
Fix
HTTPS on one domain with 301s (SEO audit T2/T3). Register in Bing Webmaster Tools and Google Search Console and submit a sitemap that includes forum topics. Enable IndexNow (via Bing) so new topics are discovered quickly.
A2Decide your AI crawler policy in robots.txtHigh
Why
Search-and-answer bots need access to cite you. Training bots are a separate choice the forum may want to make on behalf of its members.
Fix
Allow the search and answer bots: OAI-SearchBot, ChatGPT-User, PerplexityBot, Claude-SearchBot, Claude-User, Bingbot and Googlebot. Decide deliberately on the training bots (GPTBot, ClaudeBot, Google-Extended, CCBot). Members' posts are personal health stories, so explain the policy in the forum rules. Never block member-only areas by robots.txt alone; keep them behind login.
A3Establish Mensfe as a clear entityCritical
Why
AI systems build a picture of an organisation from consistent facts across many sources. Mensfe currently presents six names and no machine-readable profile.
Fix
Use one name and descriptor: "Mensfe — the men's fertility forum". Add Organization and Person schema (founders, founding date 2007, email, logo, sameAs), and author pages for Pip Reilly and Rob Barlow. Create a Wikidata item for Mensfe, and an author item for the book. Make the About, Mission and Patrons pages consistent.
A4Write the sentences you want AI to repeatCritical
Why
In our test the AI described Mensfe in its old meta description's words. Clear, factual, quotable sentences near the top of key pages tend to be what gets summarised.
Fix
Homepage opening paragraph and meta description, for example: "Mensfe is a free, anonymous peer-support forum for men facing infertility, founded in 2007 by fertility counsellor Pip Reilly and medical scientist Rob Barlow. Men discuss azoospermia, low sperm count, ICSI, donor conception and the emotional side of male infertility." Reuse the same core sentence in directory listings and social bios.
A5Structure information pages for answersHigh
Why
AI pulls short, self-contained passages that answer a question directly under a matching heading.
Fix
On each of the 12 guides: an H1, an "In short" two-to-three sentence summary, question-style H2s ("What is azoospermia?", "What tests will I have?", "What are the treatment options?"), short paragraphs, a key-facts table, sources, reviewer and date. Mark up with MedicalWebPage and BreadcrumbList. Link to the matching forum board ("Talk to men who've been through it").
A6Turn forum knowledge into citable pagesHigh
Why
AI systems cite forums heavily for "what's it really like" questions, but long threads are hard to quote. Summaries of recurring questions, linking to the source threads, give AI a clean page to cite and send people into the community.
Fix
Create 10–15 "community wisdom" pages from the most-read threads, with members' consent and anonymised quotes. Suggested topics:
  • What to ask before micro-TESE
  • Telling family about donor conception
  • How men really feel after an azoospermia diagnosis
  • Supporting your partner through male-factor IVF
  • Questions to take to your urologist
Add DiscussionForumPosting schema to topics and use descriptive topic URLs.
A7Own the "men's lived experience" nicheHigh
Why
For purely medical definitions, AI will keep preferring the NHS, NICE and clinics. Mensfe can win where those sources are thin: the male perspective, emotions, relationships and peer experience.
Fix
Lead every page with that angle. Make the founders' story visible: a medical scientist who has lived with azoospermia for over 25 years is a distinctive, citable fact. Use Pip Reilly's book (A Journey Through Male Infertility) for author interviews and podcast guest spots.
A8Earn mentions where AI already looksHigh
Why
The sources cited for support questions were Fertility Network UK, Mumsnet, BICA, Mind and clinic blogs. Being mentioned by them is the most direct route into those answers.
Fix
Request resource listings from Fertility Network UK (HIMfertility), BICA, iCSI and UK fertility clinics' "support" pages. Update the existing listings (robinhadley.co.uk, Gateway Women) to the HTTPS URL. Contribute expert comment to Men's Health Week and International Men's Day coverage. Take part, transparently, in relevant Mumsnet and Reddit discussions.
A9Show freshness and maintenanceMedium
Fix
Show "last reviewed" dates and an automatic copyright year. Put recent topics on the homepage, a news/research digest updated monthly from the Research board, and dateModified in schema.
A10Add an llms.txt fileMedium
Why
Low effort and an emerging convention, not a ranking factor. It gives AI tools a curated map of the most useful pages.
Fix
Publish /llms.txt with a one-paragraph description of Mensfe, the 12 guides, the forum boards and the community-wisdom pages (example in section 6).
A11Measure AI referralsCritical
Fix
Install GA4 and create a channel group for AI referrers (chatgpt.com, perplexity.ai, copilot.microsoft.com, gemini.google.com, claude.ai). Track forum sign-ups as a conversion, and run the monthly prompt-tracking set below.
