Critical findings

Nine issues. Each blocks indexing, measurement, or a sale.

Critical

1 · There is no sitemap

best-tech.co.uk/sitemap.xml returns a 404. Google has no index of your pages to crawl.

Cause found: in Webflow → Site settings → SEO → Sitemap, "Auto-generate sitemap" is Off (set to "Use custom sitemap.xml") and the custom sitemap box is empty. Fix: switch auto-generate On, publish, then submit the sitemap in Search Console.

Critical

2 · Not one page has an H1

All 16 pages return zero <h1> elements. The homepage hero "Fast & Reliable Mobile & Gadget Repair" is marked up as an H2. Four pages (/work, /price, /blog, /career) have no headings at all.

Fix: in the Designer, change each page's hero heading tag from H2 to H1. One H1 per page, containing the primary keyword.

Critical

3 · No canonical tags anywhere

Zero of 16 pages carry a rel="canonical" link. Webflow's Global canonical tag URL field is empty.

Fix: Site settings → SEO → Global canonical tag URL → enter https://best-tech.co.uk and save.

Critical

4 · No structured data — invisible as a local business

Zero JSON-LD blocks across the site. For a walk-in repair shop at Piccadilly this is the single biggest missed opportunity: no LocalBusiness schema means no opening hours, address, phone, price range or review stars in Google's results, and much weaker eligibility for the local map pack.

Fix: add LocalBusiness (and Service + FAQPage on the service pages) JSON-LD via Site settings → Custom code → Head. Currently the Head contains only a small style block and the Elfsight WhatsApp widget.

Critical

5 · Two pages still carry the template's own SEO copy

/price and /career both have the title "Oxygen Studio – Webflow Ecommerce website template" and the description "Oxygen Studio is a modern and high-performance agency template designed for creative studios, digital agencies, and freelancers…". That is what Google will show for your pricing page.

Fix: page settings → SEO for both pages. E.g. "Phone & Laptop Repair Prices in London | Best Tech Repair".

Critical

6 · The pricing page sends customers to someone else's website

The plan buttons on /price link out to the template author's demo sites: oxygen-studio.webflow.io/product/starter-plan, /product/custom-plan, creatix-studio.webflow.io/product/starter-plan and /product/growth-plan. Anyone clicking to buy leaves best-tech.co.uk.

Fix: repoint all four to /contact or your booking flow. Highest-value single fix on the site.

Critical

7 · Nothing is being measured

No Google Analytics 4, no Tag Manager, no Meta Pixel, no Clarity or Hotjar — the page source contains none of them. There is also no Google Search Console verification. For a performance-marketing engagement, there is currently no baseline and no conversion tracking.

Fix first, before any other change, so improvements are attributable: GA4 + Search Console, then a form/call conversion event.

Critical

8 · Mobile LCP is 9.0 seconds

Driven by a 4K (3840×2160) hero video weighing 5.78 MB, plus a 1.28 MB uncompressed JPEG and ~1,150 ms of render-blocking requests.

Fix: re-encode the hero to 1280×720 (~800 KB), add a poster image, and don't autoplay it on mobile. Re-upload the laptop JPEG as WebP.

Critical

9 · Layout shift of 0.319 on mobile

Three times Google's "poor" boundary. Content jumps under the reader's thumb while the page loads — Lighthouse flags both layout-shift culprits and forced reflow.

Fix: set explicit width/height on images, and reserve space for the hero video and the Elfsight chat widget.

High-priority findings

Eleven issues actively costing rankings or customer trust.

High

10 · Five pages share one title, four share one description

/, /about, /work, /blog and /contact are all titled just "Best Tech Repair" (16 characters — Google allows ~60). On four of them the meta description is also the literal string "Best Tech Repair".

Fix: unique 50–60 character titles and 140–160 character descriptions per page. The nine service pages already do this well — copy that pattern.

High

11 · Every page carries a web-agency tagline

The footer on all 16 pages reads "We are a creative agency that blends strategy, design, and technology to craft digital experiences that inspire and drive growth" — leftover template copy describing a design studio, not a repair shop.

Fix: one edit to the footer symbol in the Designer updates the whole site.

High

12 · The 404 page is still entirely the template

Title: "Oxygen Studio – Webflow Ecommerce website template". Its menu shows "New York, NY / Remote", "+1 (234) 567-8910" and "hello@creatixStudio.com". A UK customer who mistypes a URL sees an American web agency.

Fix: rebuild the 404 with your nav, your Piccadilly address and 020 3417 7090, plus links to the main service pages.

High

13 · 106 of 157 images have no alt text

Only 32% coverage. The four /works/ service pages are at 0/10 — not a single image described. This costs image search, accessibility, and keyword context on exactly the pages meant to rank.

Fix: alt text in the Asset panel applies everywhere the asset is used — do it once per asset, not per placement.

High

14 · robots.txt is empty

Served with a 200 status and zero bytes. Crawlers are allowed, but there's no Sitemap: directive pointing them anywhere.

Fix: once the sitemap exists, add Sitemap: https://best-tech.co.uk/sitemap.xml in Site settings → SEO → robots.txt.

High

15 · Social links are broken

Sitewide footer links go to facebook.com (the homepage, not a profile) and www.instragram.com — a typo for a domain that isn't Instagram. Four further footer link blocks point at #.

High

16 · No Search Console verification

The Google site verification field in Webflow is empty, so there's no visibility of impressions, queries, or indexing errors — and no way to submit the sitemap.

High

17 · The blog is live with zero posts

/blog is published, linked in both menus, and returns no collection items. It's a dead end for visitors and a thin page for Google (497 words, mostly nav).

Fix: either unpublish it until there's content, or publish 3–4 posts targeting real queries ("iPhone screen repair cost London", "is it worth repairing a 5-year-old laptop").

High

18 · Store currency is set to US dollars

Webflow Ecommerce is configured with "currencyCode":"USD" and a $ symbol on a UK site quoting UK phone numbers and a London address.

High

19 · Header and footer menus disagree

Header: Home · About us · Work · Pricing · Blogs · carrier · Contact. Footer: Home · Services · About · Book Repair · Contact. The same /work page is called "Work" in one and "Services" in the other, and the footer omits pricing entirely.

High

20 · Spelling errors in visible copy

Homepage heading: "We Fix everything quick & corrctly First Time". Main navigation: "carrier" where it should read "Careers".

Where to start

Ordered by impact per hour of work. The first four are settings changes, not builds.

  1. Turn on the sitemap and set the canonical URL. Site settings → SEO. Two toggles, then publish. Roughly five minutes and it unblocks indexing.
  2. Install GA4 and verify Search Console. Do this before anything else changes, so every later improvement is measurable. Verification ID goes in the same SEO panel.
  3. Repoint the four pricing buttons away from the template demo sites to your own booking page. This is live revenue leaking today.
  4. Clear the template residue: the /price and /career SEO fields, the footer tagline, the 404 page, the Instagram typo, and the USD currency setting.
  5. Change hero headings from H2 to H1 on all 16 pages, and write unique titles and descriptions for the seven pages that share them.
  6. Re-encode the hero video to 720p with a poster image, and convert the 1.28 MB JPEG to WebP. Expect mobile LCP to drop from 9.0s toward the 2.5s target.
  7. Add LocalBusiness schema with the Piccadilly address, 020 3417 7090, and the 10am–10pm daily hours already on the site.
  8. Work through alt text in the Asset panel — start with the four /works/ pages, which have none at all.

Method & limitations

All 16 pages were fetched and parsed directly from the live site on 13 August 2026; every count in this report comes from that parse rather than an estimate. Performance figures are a single Lighthouse mobile lab run via PageSpeed Insights on the homepage — lab runs vary by a few points between runs, and no real-user field data exists yet for this domain. Webflow settings were read from Site settings → SEO and → Custom code. Asset sizes were measured by downloading the files. Not covered: backlink profile, keyword rankings, competitor comparison, Google Business Profile, and the CMS collection structure inside the Designer.
